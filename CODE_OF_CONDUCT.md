# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc].

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, but call out behaviour that crosses the line.
- Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent to
`conduct@tidewall.ai`. Reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
